Riviera — Screenshots

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Riviera is an independent iOS companion app for Renault connected vehicles: fuel and range, where you parked, horn and lights, lease and ownership costs, service reminders and fuel records, on your iPhone.

These screenshots show the app's built-in Demo Mode with sample data. They are not a real person's car or account.
