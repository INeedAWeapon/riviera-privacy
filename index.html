Riviera — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

The short version: Riviera has no servers of its own, no analytics, no trackers, and no advertising. Your records stay on your iPhone and leave it only in features you choose to use (iCloud Sync, Apple TV, Shared Garage, exported backups and reports, Calendar and Reminders entries, and buttons or Shortcuts that hand a place, your car's position or your VIN to another app or website, such as Share, Copy, directions and the recall check) or inside an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone, if you make one. To work, the app talks directly to Renault, Apple and a few public map, weather and fuel-price services, listed below. The app never sends anything to the developer by itself. Apart from anonymous crash and usage reports that Apple passes on if you opted in to share them, feedback sent through TestFlight, and anything you choose to send (such as an e-mail), the developer receives nothing.

Who we are

Riviera is an independent, community-built iOS companion app for Renault connected vehicles, made by an individual developer. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Renault Group or any of its subsidiaries. Contact details are at the end of this page.

Features that use your iCloud

iCloud Sync and Apple TV are separate switches, off until you turn them on. Shared Garage has no switch: it starts when you tap Share Garage… (or open an invitation). All of them use Apple's iCloud under your own Apple ID; none uses a server run by the developer.

  • iCloud Sync keeps your records, your car's name, the places you have named (with their map positions, to about 10 metres) and settings such as language, country, currency, units and alert choices in step across your own devices, through your private iCloud container. The scanned files themselves are not part of it. Records you delete stay in these copies as hidden "deleted" entries that keep their original contents, until you use Delete All My Data (which removes them from your own iCloud copies, not from other devices).
  • Riviera on Apple TV. If you switch on Show my car on Apple TV (Settings → Data & Sync), Riviera saves one small summary of your car in your iCloud key-value storage so the Riviera app on an Apple TV signed in to the same Apple ID can show it: the car's name as you typed it, its model and year, the address of its picture (left out if it contains your VIN), range, fuel or battery level, charging and lock status (with any open doors), odometer, your lease progress (kilometres left and pace, never money), the next service, up to six of your tracked documents or services (such as insurance or inspection), each with the name you gave it and when it is due, your theme colours, language and units, your car's drive type, and the times the car and the phone last reported. You can separately choose to include the street address where the car is parked and how long it has been there (Include Parking Location); coordinates are never shared. This works whether or not iCloud Sync is on. The summary is stored by Apple in your iCloud and is not sent to, or readable by, the developer. The Apple TV app only displays it and cannot control your car. It shows this on the television screen, so anyone who can see the screen can read it, including the parked street address if you turned that on, and the summary is readable by every Apple TV signed in to the same Apple ID.
    Signing out, switching account or entering Demo Mode takes the summary off iCloud but leaves the switches as you set them, so it is published again at the next sign-in. Turning sharing off removes the summary and turns off both Show my car on Apple TV and Include Parking Location on this iPhone; Delete All My Data removes it as well and also resets those two switches and the iCloud Sync switch; another iPhone on the same Apple ID that has sharing on will publish it again until you turn it off there. If your iPhone cannot reach iCloud whenever Riviera tries to take the summary off (signing out, switching account, entering Demo Mode, turning sharing off or Delete All My Data), it retries the next time the app opens or comes to the foreground, or when the iCloud account changes. Deleting the app alone does not remove it.
  • Shared Garage. When you tap Share Garage…, Riviera stores a full copy of that car's records (including prices, lease and bank-credit details, and subscription entries such as insurance and driving-licence dates), the car's name (which also titles the invitation), the VIN, and all the places you have named, for all your cars, with their map positions (to about 10 metres), in your private CloudKit database. While the garage stays linked on this iPhone, Riviera updates that copy automatically a couple of seconds after you add or change records, and merges the changes your invitees make into your records on this device. It stays there, even if you cancel or stop sharing, until you use Delete All My Data. The Apple IDs you invite can read and change it; it cannot be made view-only. If you accept someone else's invitation, you can merge their records into one of your cars on this device. The shared copy stays in their iCloud, not yours, and whatever you hold for that car (its records, its name and VIN) is written back into the shared copy, together with all the places you have named, for all your cars, with their positions (to about 10 metres), so the owner and everyone they have invited can see it. Records you delete stay in the shared copy as hidden "deleted" entries that keep their original contents, which everyone with access to that copy can read.

Diagnostics

Riviera keeps a diagnostics log on your device so that a problem can be understood without asking you to reproduce it. It holds the most recent 160 app events (including lock readings and the times of remote commands and when the app launched or moved between the foreground and background) and up to 24 recent visits, with the time of each, to a handful of screens such as Controls, Consumption, Parking History and the maps.

Riviera never uploads this log, and it leaves your iPhone only if you choose to send it, or inside a backup of your iPhone (iCloud or computer) if you make one. Riviera has no server. Three things can send it, and each only acts when you tap it: Export Redacted Diagnostics, Report a Problem and the Send Report button on the "Something went wrong" card. Export lets you choose where the file goes; Report a Problem and Send Report prepare an e-mail addressed to the developer (riviera.appsupport@icloud.com), and nothing is sent until you send it. Export Redacted Diagnostics and Report a Problem show you the complete file first. Send Report builds the e-mail text itself instead of attaching a file: the same diagnostics, plus the time and the text of the error that triggered the card (redacted the same way); if no mail app can open the draft, it copies that text to your clipboard instead. These e-mails are sent from your own mail account, so the developer also sees the address and name you send from and anything you write; the redaction applies only to the diagnostics text.

The file you send holds the recorded events and screen visits plus your iOS version, your iPhone's model identifier (for example iPhone17,2) and screen size, the app version and build, your country, language and units, whether iCloud Sync, Beta and Demo Mode are on, a summary of your car's model, year, powertrain and connected services, the car's last reported lock and door state with its time, the times of unlock reminders and alerts and whether that alert is switched on, the names and end dates of its Renault connected-service contracts, whether its lock and remote commands work, its last eight remote commands with their times, how many automatic Renault reads the app has made recently, and, in some event lines, the address path of a Renault request (with the VIN and account identifiers masked).

Before an event is saved, Riviera removes anything that looks like an e-mail address, a VIN, a sign-in token or a coordinate pair, and any account or person identifier, whether labelled (such as accountId=) or part of a Renault web address. It also removes the name you gave the car that was selected when the app started, if that name is at least three characters long. A rename, switching to another car or a first sign-in is covered only after the app is restarted. The app never logs your password and is written to avoid logging names, places and street addresses. The removal above covers only the items it lists: it does not recognise other names, place names or street addresses, and because it works by pattern it cannot recognise free text, so read the file before you send it.

You can read the recorded events at any time in Settings → Help & Support → Diagnostics → Diagnostics Log, and the complete file in the review step of Export Redacted Diagnostics. You can clear the log from the Diagnostics screen. When you export or report, Riviera also leaves the file it created in the app's cache folder (excluded from backups) until iOS clears it or you run Delete All My Data; Clear Diagnostics does not remove that copy. Delete All My Data removes the log and that file along with everything else.

Where the app sends network requests

Riviera communicates only with:

  • Renault's services — the Gigya sign-in and the Kamereon vehicle API, to operate your car's connected features (vehicle data, and the commands you send); Renault's public websites for the dealer locator and the accessories catalogue (the dealer locator receives a position rounded to about 100 metres and a search radius, the accessories catalogue receives your car's model codes, the country site you reach shows your market, and neither receives your VIN); and the web address Renault's API supplies for your car's picture, from which the picture is downloaded (the Apple TV app downloads it too, if you use it). These websites and the picture's host also see your IP address, as with any web request, and the picture is requested from exactly the web address Renault's API supplies. When the iPhone downloads the picture it does not check the address for your VIN or remove anything from it, so if the address contains an identifier such as your VIN, the server that serves the picture receives it. The only check is on the copy of the address saved for Apple TV, which is left out if it contains the VIN;
  • Public data providers — fuel prices (national open-data services in France, Spain, Austria and Italy; Tankerkönig in Germany if you add your own key in Settings, otherwise OpenStreetMap; PretCarburant.ro in Romania), fuel stations, EV chargers and dealers (Open Charge Map and OpenStreetMap data, the latter through the public Overpass servers overpass-api.de, overpass.kumi.systems and overpass.openstreetmap.fr, tried in turn) and weather (Open-Meteo). Searches by area carry a position rounded to about 100 metres: your car's parked position, the centre of the map you are viewing or, for the dealer locator and the EV charger search, your phone's position (if you have allowed location). Spain's and Italy's fuel data are whole-country downloads that carry no position. These requests never carry your name, e-mail, VIN or plate, and, as with any web request, the provider can see your IP address. Your own Tankerkönig key, if you add one in Settings, is kept in the Keychain and sent to Tankerkönig with German fuel searches; without it, German stations come from OpenStreetMap through Overpass. Riviera includes its own Open Charge Map key, the same for every user, so it does not identify you to Open Charge Map;
  • Apple services — iCloud (including the optional features above) and Apple Maps. Riviera asks Apple's geocoder to turn your car's parked position, and the places in Parking History, into a street address, and uses Apple Maps to draw maps and to search for dealers. Unlike the public providers above, Apple receives exact coordinates for these (the search centre, your car's parked spots and, if you have allowed location, your phone's position once at first sign-in, to find your country). Apple's own privacy policy applies.

While the app is closed, a background refresh (at most about every 15 minutes, when iOS allows it) may contact Renault and update your widgets. If you use iCloud Sync, Shared Garage or Apple TV sharing, it may also update those iCloud copies, for example with a refill it detected and saved automatically. While notifications are allowed, it may also contact Open-Meteo (when a weather alert is on; the hail alert is on by default) and, when your fuel is low and the low-fuel alert is on (it is by default), a fuel-price provider for the nearest station, using your car's parked position rounded as above. Riviera never tracks your phone's location in the background.

Notifications and Lock Screen cards are created on your device. Riviera does not register for or send push notifications and has no server that could send them; iCloud itself may use Apple's push service to tell your devices that synced data changed.

Some buttons open a website or another app: for example Renault pages, official vehicle portals, dealer websites, Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze for directions, Apple Weather, the phone dialler or your mail app. Those requests are made by your browser or that app, not by Riviera, and are governed by their own policies. Directions and weather buttons hand the exact position of your car or the place to that app (directions in Apple Maps or Google Maps also carry the name you gave the car; Waze and Apple Weather receive only the position), and the recall check adds your VIN to the link so Renault's page can pre-fill it. To offer your preferred maps app, Riviera checks whether Google Maps or Waze is installed.

Share and Copy buttons (the parked spot, the VIN, an address, a backup, a report) hand exactly what you chose to the iOS share sheet or clipboard, and where it goes next is up to you. Sharing the parked spot includes its exact coordinates. Riviera writes to the clipboard only when you tap a Copy button, or when you tap Send Report and no mail app can open the draft (it then copies the report text, which includes the redacted diagnostics). It never reads the clipboard.

iOS keeps a cache of the answers from the public providers, from Renault's public websites (the dealer locator and the accessories catalogue) and from the server that supplies your car's picture, on your device. The request addresses in it can contain the rounded position (dealer locator and map searches), your car's model codes (accessories), your VIN if the web address Renault supplies for your car's picture contains it, and, for German fuel searches, your own Tankerkönig key; cookies those sites set are stored and sent back on later requests. Only the Renault sign-in and vehicle-data sessions (Gigya and Kamereon) are not cached or stored this way. Delete All My Data clears both the cache and the cookies.

There are no Riviera servers. No analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs are embedded, and the app contains zero third-party code dependencies. Separately from Riviera, Apple may give the developer anonymous crash reports and usage figures for the app (for example downloads and sessions) if you have switched on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, and the feedback, screenshots and crash reports that TestFlight testers choose to send.

Your controls

  • Delete everything: Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete All My Data removes the data Riviera stores on the iPhone (your records, your car's position history, preferences, cached images and files, and any exported backups or reports left in temporary storage), Keychain entries, widget data, pending and delivered notifications, the diagnostics log, your iCloud Drive sync file and iCloud key-value copy, the Apple TV summary, and the Shared Garage copy you own in your iCloud (CloudKit) together with its invitation, which ends everyone's access to it. It also removes the Calendar events and Reminders whose identifiers Riviera saved, if Riviera has full access to Calendar and Reminders; any it cannot reach stay where they are, and you can delete them in Calendar or Reminders.
  • What it does not remove: your Renault account and the data Renault holds; requests already received by Apple and the public map, weather and fuel-price services, which keep their own logs; backups of your iPhone; backup or report files you saved elsewhere, and anything you copied to the clipboard; copies on other devices (a device that still has iCloud Sync on can upload its data again) or held by people you shared with (anyone who already merged a Shared Garage keeps their copy on their device); and a Shared Garage you joined, which Riviera cannot leave for you: ask its owner to remove you and what you added. A few non-personal markers also stay on the iPhone: the Demo Mode setting, one-time migration markers and a Renault rate-limit timer. If an erase could not confirm that a copy in iCloud was removed, a marker saying so also stays, so the app can warn you again.
  • If Riviera cannot confirm that a copy in iCloud was removed, it shows a warning that a copy may remain. It stays on screen for about 12 seconds (longer with VoiceOver) unless you tap it away. When the iPhone is signed out of iCloud, it only warns if its own records show you used iCloud Sync, Apple TV sharing or a linked Shared Garage, so it cannot warn about a Shared Garage you started and then cancelled or stopped sharing, because it keeps no record of it. When you are signed in to iCloud but offline, it always tries to remove the Shared Garage copy and warns if that fails. For the iCloud Sync files and key-value copy it can only check this iPhone: if the iPhone is offline, iCloud finishes the removal when it next connects. To be sure, run Delete All My Data while signed in to iCloud and online. After an erase the app returns to the sign-in screen, and Settings opens again once you sign in or open the sample car.
  • Export your records: Settings → Data & Sync → Export Backup produces a portable file of the selected car's records, as described in the table above. The position history, the separate odometer log, the EV charge log, the command list and your settings are not in the Export Backup file or the history reports; you can see most of them in the app. (The redacted diagnostics file you can export lists your last eight remote commands and a few settings, as described under Diagnostics.) Scans and other attached documents are included only if you turn on Include documents (off by default).
  • Lock Screen privacy: Show address on Lock Screen (Settings → Privacy & Security) is off by default. While it is off, the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island card, widgets and StandBy name the parked spot only with a label you gave it, such as "Home"; the Apple Watch and CarPlay card shows no place. (Riviera has no CarPlay or Apple Watch app of its own; iOS can show its widgets and Live Activities there.) The car's name, fuel level, range, lock status, service-due text, consumption, lease progress and the time it has been parked can still be seen while the phone is locked, and a low-fuel notification can name the nearest station. Notifications can show more while the phone is locked, depending on the alerts you have on: your car's name; the name and due date of a tracked document or payment (for example insurance); lease and bank-credit dates and payment days; how much of your mileage allowance you have used and an estimated excess-mileage charge in your currency; a service that is due; the time your parking meter runs out; that your electric car has reached its charge target, with the charge level; weather at your car, including winter or summer tyre-season advice based on the temperature there; and that the car has been left unlocked for over 15 minutes. Notification reminders (not entries in the Reminders app) are queued with iOS when you save an item. A monthly payment's reminders cover the next 12 months and are topped up daily. A one-off document reminder is queued for the number of days you chose before the document's expiry date, which can be years away. A weekly, monthly or yearly repeating reminder stays queued until you delete the item or switch the Documents and services alert off. Lease and bank-credit reminders, which contain the car's name and contract dates, are queued for the dates leading up to the end of the term, with lease payment reminders for the next three instalments. Queued reminders stay in iOS's notification storage until they fire, you change, renew, mark as paid or delete the item or contract, you switch off the alert they belong to in Riviera's notification settings (Documents and services, Contract expiry, Lease & credit alerts or Parking meter reminder), or you use Delete All My Data. To hide the text while the phone is locked, set Show Previews to When Unlocked for Riviera in iOS Settings.
  • Siri, Shortcuts and controls: Riviera's Siri phrases and Shortcuts actions that only read data (the car summary, fuel or battery level, range, lock state, service and inspection dates, lease kilometres, tyre pressure) answer from the data already stored on your iPhone, without contacting Renault. With App Lock on, the service, inspection, lease and tyre answers are withheld. Where is my car needs the phone unlocked; the others can be answered while it is locked if you allow Siri on the Lock Screen in iOS Settings. Actions and Control Center or Lock Screen controls that send a command to your car (flash lights, horn, pre-conditioning) contact Renault and need the phone unlocked. Where is my car also hands the parked position, as exact coordinates, to Shortcuts (even when Siri does not say the address), so a shortcut you build can pass it to other actions or apps. The Open Parking control only opens the app.
  • iCloud Sync, Apple TV, Shared Garage, Calendar and Reminders are opt-in. Switching off Apple TV sharing removes the Apple TV summary from your iCloud. Switching off iCloud Sync stops further uploads but leaves existing iCloud copies in place, and so does ending a Shared Garage you own; those stay until you use Delete All My Data. Calendar and Reminders are used only if you grant access and add an event or reminder yourself; after that, Riviera updates or removes the ones it created when you change or delete the matching service item.
  • App Lock (Face ID / Touch ID / passcode) is optional. It covers the app itself: the app asks again after you have been away for more than 30 seconds, and it cannot work on a phone with no passcode, in which case the app opens and says so. It does not hide widgets, Live Activities, Lock Screen cards or notifications: anyone holding the locked iPhone can read what they show unless you hide it in iOS Settings, and iOS can also show the widgets and cards on CarPlay or Apple Watch. It covers only part of what Siri and Shortcuts can say: the car summary, fuel or battery level, range and lock state can still be spoken while the iPhone is locked if you allow Siri on the Lock Screen, while the service, inspection, lease and tyre answers are withheld while App Lock is on. GDPR

Riviera has no developer-side database. Your data is held on your device (and in any backup of your iPhone), by Renault for your connected-car account, and in the iCloud, Apple TV, Calendar, Reminders and Shared Garage features you choose to use. When a feature needs it, your car's position is sent to Apple as exact coordinates and, rounded to about 100 metres and together with your IP address, to Renault's dealer locator and to the public map, weather and fuel-price services listed above. For dealer and charger searches your phone's position is handled the same way. The one-time country check sends your phone's position to Apple only. Those services keep their own records under their own policies. You can see most of the data Riviera holds in the app (not the hidden "deleted" entries), export the records you entered as an Export Backup file (car by car; that file leaves out the position history, the separate odometer log, the EV charge log, the command list and settings, although odometer values inside your fuel, service, repair and lease records are included), and erase it, as described above, without asking the developer. For data in your Renault account, contact Renault. For anything you e-mailed to riviera.appsupport@icloud.com, write to that address and the developer will delete it on request. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.
