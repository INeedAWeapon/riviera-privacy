Riviera — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 June 2026

The short version: Riviera has no servers, no analytics, no trackers, and no advertising. Your data stays on your device and, if you enable sync, in your personal iCloud. Nothing is ever sent to the developer.

Who we are

Riviera is an independent, community-built iOS companion app for Renault connected vehicles. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Renault Group or any of its subsidiaries.

Where the app sends network requests

Riviera communicates only with:

  • Renault's official services (Gigya sign-in and the Kamereon vehicle API) — to operate your car's connected features;
  • Public data providers — fuel prices (national open-data services, Tankerkönig, PretCarburant.ro), EV chargers (Open Charge Map, OpenStreetMap), dealers (Renault's public dealer locator, Apple Maps, OpenStreetMap), weather (OpenWeather), and safety recalls. These requests contain coordinates of the searched map area, never your identity;
  • Apple services — Maps, iCloud, and push notifications, governed by Apple's privacy policy.

There are no Riviera servers. No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are embedded. The app contains zero third-party code dependencies.

Your controls

  • Delete everything: Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete All My Data removes all local data, Keychain entries, widget data, and iCloud-synced files.
  • Export everything: Settings → Advanced → Export Backup produces a complete, portable copy of your records.
  • iCloud Sync is opt-in and can be turned off at any time.
  • App Lock (Face ID / Touch ID / passcode) is available and optional.

GDPR

Because all personal data is stored on your device or in your personal iCloud account, you remain in direct control of it. The rights of access, portability, and erasure are exercised entirely through the in-app Export and Delete functions described above — there is no developer-side database to request data from.

Children

Riviera is intended for vehicle owners and is not directed at children under 16.
